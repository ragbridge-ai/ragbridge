# Acme Cloud — Onboarding Guide

**Day one.** On their first day, new employees receive a laptop, a company email address, and access to the internal wiki. IT support is available in the #it-help Slack channel.

**Product access.** New hires get access to Acme Cloud's own product within their first week, so they can explore the dashboard from a customer's perspective before starting product-specific training.

**Buddy program.** Every new employee is paired with a buddy from a different team for their first 30 days, to help them navigate the company outside their own department.

**30-60-90 day plan.** Managers and new hires agree on a 30-60-90 day plan during the first week, with concrete goals reviewed at each milestone.

**Benefits enrollment.** New employees have 30 days from their start date to enroll in health insurance and other benefits; missing this window means waiting until the next open enrollment period.
