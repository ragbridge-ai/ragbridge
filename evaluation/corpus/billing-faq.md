# Acme Cloud — Billing FAQ

**Billing cycle.** Acme Cloud bills customers monthly, on the anniversary of their signup date. Annual plans are billed once per year and include a 15% discount.

**Payment methods.** Acme Cloud accepts credit cards and ACH bank transfers. Wire transfers are available only for annual plans above $10,000.

**Invoices.** Invoices are emailed automatically on the billing date and are also available for download from the billing portal at any time.

**Failed payments.** If a payment fails, Acme Cloud retries it three times over seven days before suspending the account. Customers are notified by email after each failed attempt.

**Taxes.** Sales tax is calculated automatically based on the billing address on file. Customers with tax-exempt status must upload a valid exemption certificate.
