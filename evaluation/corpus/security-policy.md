# Acme Cloud — Security Policy

**Passwords.** Acme Cloud requires passwords to be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords must be changed every 180 days.

**Two-factor authentication.** Two-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts with administrator access. Employees can use an authenticator app or a hardware security key.

**Data retention.** Customer data is retained for 90 days after account cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted. Customers can request earlier deletion by contacting support.

**Incident response.** Any suspected security incident must be reported to security@acmecloud.example within one hour of discovery. Acme Cloud aims to notify affected customers within 72 hours of confirming a breach.

**Access reviews.** Access to production systems is reviewed quarterly. Employees who change teams or leave the company have their access revoked within 24 hours.
