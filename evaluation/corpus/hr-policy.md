# Acme Cloud — HR Policy

**Vacation.** Full-time employees at Acme Cloud receive 22 days of paid vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused vacation days roll over into the next year, up to a maximum of 10 days.

**Sick leave.** Employees may take up to 12 paid sick days per year without a doctor's note. A doctor's note is required only for absences longer than three consecutive days.

**Remote work.** Acme Cloud allows employees to work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a manager and from HR.

**Parental leave.** New parents receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave, regardless of gender or whether the child arrived through birth, adoption, or fostering.

**Expense reimbursement.** Business expenses under $75 can be submitted through the expense portal without a manager's approval. Expenses of $75 or more require manager sign-off before reimbursement.
